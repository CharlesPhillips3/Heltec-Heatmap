Heltec Heatmap Flasher

Installs the Wi‑Fi survey firmware on a Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 from Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari and iPhone cannot flash boards.

Pick your board

This erases the board, including Meshtastic. You can put Meshtastic back later from flasher.meshtastic.org.

Steps

  1. Plug the Heltec into the computer with a USB‑C data cable.
  2. Click your board above and pick the serial port that appears.
  3. If it will not connect: hold PRG, tap RST, let go of PRG, and try again.
  4. When it finishes, press RST. The screen shows Heltec-Heatmap-XXXX.
  5. Then open the Heltec Heatmap app on your iPhone.
